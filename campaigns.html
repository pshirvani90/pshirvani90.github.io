FMCG & personal care | Digital, experiential and influencer marketing

Campaign Case Summaries

A selection of FMCG and personal care activation campaigns focused on digital, experiential, and influencer marketing.

Nescafe Ice: Product Launch Campaign

FMCG | Digital Launch Nescafe Ice entered the Iranian market for the first time as a summer-ready alternative to hot coffee.

What Was Done

  • Supported digital launch planning and rollout on Instagram
  • Helped shape an emotional storytelling approach positioning the product as a summer lifestyle companion
  • Supported content coordination and campaign sequencing (teaser → reveal → engagement)

Outcome

Strong audience excitement and engagement around product launch Successful introduction of a new product line during peak summer season

SunStar: Interactive Product Launch

FMCG | Digital + Experiential Activation SunStar launched new party-style drinks in three flavors, aiming to communicate a fresh, energetic brand mood.

What Was Done

  • Supported integrated launch combining Instagram content and offline activation
  • Assisted with coordination of an interactive projection-mapping installation
  • Helped align offline experience with digital storytelling

Outcome

High visibility launch with strong brand recall Interactive installation increased curiosity and on-site engagement

Neuderm: “Between the Sun and Me”

Personal Care | Influencer Marketing Campaign Neuderm introduced new sunscreen products in a highly competitive summer skincare market.

What Was Done

  • Supported influencer strategy across beauty, lifestyle, and health creators
  • Helped coordinate messaging around sun protection and product benefits
  • Assisted with campaign rollout and performance monitoring

Outcome

High reach and credibility through trusted influencers Strong positioning of Neuderm as a reliable and accessible sunscreen brand

Schauma | Shampoo Rollers Activation

FMCG | Experiential Sampling Schauma needed a bold approach to increase brand awareness beyond traditional sampling.

What Was Done

  • Supported a large-scale experiential sampling concept using mobile “Shampoo Rollers”
  • Assisted with execution planning and on-ground activation coordination
  • Helped turn sampling into a shareable brand experience

Outcome

300,000+ samples distributed High visibility and strong audience interaction in public spaces
