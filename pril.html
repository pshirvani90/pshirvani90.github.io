Pril

Integrated Brand & Activation Campaigns | Experiential, Digital & Influencer Marketing

Pril is a core Henkel brand with a strong focus on experiential activation, influencer marketing, and digital storytelling. Across multiple campaigns, the objective was to strengthen emotional connection with consumers, drive product trial, and reinforce Pril’s role in everyday shared moments, both online and offline.

My Role Across Pril Campaigns

I supported multiple Pril campaigns as part of a cross-functional agency team, collaborating with brand managers, creative, production, activation (BTL), and influencer teams. I contributed to activation strategy development and execution, supporting planning decisions, aligning messaging with brand objectives, and tracking performance across digital and experiential touchpoints.

Selected Campaigns

Smart Sampling “Enjoy Together”

Focus: Experiential activation, BTL, consumer engagement

  • Supported planning and execution of a gamified smart sampling experience designed to increase engagement beyond traditional product distribution
  • Coordinated interactive booth elements and user journey across multiple locations
  • Integrated surveys, AR touchpoints, and sampling to enable data collection and post-campaign insights

New Gel Launch

Focus: Influencer & digital campaign execution

  • Supported influencer collaboration strategy for product re-introduction
  • Assisted with coordination of digital assets and messaging consistency
  • Contributed to campaign performance tracking and reporting

“Enjoy Together” Influencer Series

Focus: Influencer marketing & community engagement

  • Supported collaboration with chef KOL and lifestyle influencers
  • Coordinated content flow across IGTV, social posts, and UGC initiatives
  • Helped sustain engagement across multiple phases of the campaign

Big Size Campaign

Focus: Content strategy & digital storytelling

  • Supported content planning to reintroduce an existing product line with renewed relevance
  • Helped align messaging around value, sustainability, and everyday convenience
  • Assisted with digital rollout coordination across social media channels

Performance & Impact

  • Multi-location experiential activations with high consumer engagement
  • Thousands of consumer interactions across BTL and digital touchpoints
  • Influencer-led reach supporting sustained brand visibility
  • Consistent alignment with Pril’s brand mission of shared joy and everyday connection

Key Learnings

  1. Experience-led sampling drives stronger engagement and recall than traditional distribution
  2. Influencer continuity strengthens trust and long-term brand connection
  3. Integrated BTL and digital execution creates more complete consumer journeys
  4. Reframing existing products can unlock renewed relevance and impact
