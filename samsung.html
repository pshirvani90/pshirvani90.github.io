CASE STUDY

Samsung

CSR & Brand Activation Campaigns

SAMSUNG

Technology Brand | CSR, Digital & Integrated Campaigns

Samsung regularly engages its audience through brand-led and CSR initiatives designed to connect technology with human values. Across multiple campaigns, the objective was to reinforce emotional relevance, promote social responsibility, and deliver impactful storytelling through digital and community-driven executions.

My Role Across Samsung Campaigns

I supported multiple Samsung campaigns as part of a cross-functional agency team, collaborating with brand managers, creative, strategy, production, activation, and influencer teams. My role focused on supporting integrated campaign planning and execution, helping align messaging, digital content, and audience engagement strategies across both brand and CSR initiatives.

Selected Campaigns

“Stay in Love” (Valentine’s Day Campaign)

Focus: Creative storytelling & digital content

  • Supported development and rollout of a Valentine’s Day mini-campaign
  • Assisted with coordination of visual content using bullet-time techniques
  • Helped align familiar product use-cases with emotional storytelling

New Year / Nowruz Campaign

Focus: Brand storytelling & digital engagement

  • Supported a culturally driven campaign centered on hope and togetherness
  • Coordinated digital rollout and messaging alignment across Instagram channels
  • Assisted with content planning and performance tracking

“No Borders with Nature” (CSR Campaign)

Focus: CSR, environmental awareness & audience engagement

  • Supported campaign execution focused on sustainability and plastic reduction
  • Coordinated messaging across social and landing page experiences
  • Assisted with engagement tracking and audience participation initiatives

Samsung AUT Tech Formation Camp

Focus: CSR, community building & influencer support

  • Supported campaign planning to attract early-stage entrepreneurs
  • Coordinated influencer collaborations targeting startup and tech audiences
  • Assisted with content flow and campaign reporting

Performance & Impact (Across Campaigns)

  • Multi-campaign digital reach across national brand initiatives
  • High engagement on Instagram through creative and CSR-led content
  • Strong audience participation across storytelling and community campaign

Key Learnings

  1. Emotion-driven storytelling strengthens brand relevance in technology categories
  2. CSR campaigns benefit from participatory and community-focused execution
  3. Cultural context significantly enhances campaign resonance
  4. Integrated digital execution ensures consistency across brand initiatives
